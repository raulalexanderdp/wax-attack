Grip & Gaffer Package

FOR SCOUT DISCUSSION  ·  REV 1, 16 OCT 2026
The number that drives this list. At 14:30 the sun delivers about 8,700 fc. A 1.2k HMI through a 6×6 at 6 ft delivers about 167 fc — 5.7 stops under. No unit in a 1-ton package keys against that sun. So for Set 1 the sun is the key and we shape it with bounce; the 1.2k's real job is Set 2, after the sun leaves. Levels are order-of-magnitude from luminous flux and inverse square — check against the head's own photometric chart.

Design intent

SET 1 · BAND · hard side sunKey = the sun, camera left, 43.5°→31.6°. It is the only source even across 14 ft. Fill the shadow side with bounce to ~4:1. Put a low bounce under the frame line to get light into eyes under the cap brims — that is the real problem at 43°, not the ratio.
SET 2 · DUO · sun leaving16:30–17:00 natural — raking key camera left at 18.7°→12°, bounce right for fill. This is the best light of the day; front-load hero coverage. 17:00–17:15 crossover. 17:15 on: 1.2k through 6×6, camera left at the sun's departing angle so the key direction does not change on the cut. Backlight for separation once the sky goes flat.
