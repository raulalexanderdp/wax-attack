Lighting Plan

REV 2, 16 OCT 2026  ·  SUN IS CAMERA LEFT ALL AFTERNOON  ·  KEY DIRECTION NEVER FLIPS
ONE RIG PER SET. IT DOES NOT MOVE DURING THE SET. Cameras reposition and change lenses; the light stays where it is. Between passes we walk the bounces in — same gear, shorter distance. Nothing is built, struck or re-rigged while the band is playing.
WORKING STOP — ISO 800, 1/50 (180° at 24p). 14:30 ND2.1 → f/6.3–6.7  ·  16:30 ND1.8 → f/8  ·  17:00 ND1.5 → f/10  ·  17:30 ND0.9 → f/14, then pull ND and open. 6–8 stops of IRND or we don't shoot.

Set 1 · Band · 14:30–15:30 · sun camera left 43.5°→31.6°

BASE RIG — set by 14:25, stays all set2 STANDS · 8 BAGS · 10 MIN
KEYSun, camera left. Hard, 90° side. The only source even across 14 ft.8,735 fc
FILL2× 8×8 ultrabounce, camera right at 12 ft, in full sun — one per half of the band. Just outside the 35 mm frustum, so they serve every lens.942 fc
EYEThe concrete. Do not carpet under the band. Bare pad returns ~1,050 fc of uplight into faces — that is what opens the cap-brim shadow, it covers all four players, and it cannot be in shot because it is the shot. Carpet drops it to 150 fc and the eyes go black. Carpet only where a hot spot reads in the background.1,052 fc
RATIOTarget 4:1. Meter at the vocalist's mark before the first take and leave it alone.

NOTENo overhead on the band. A 6×6 covers one person, not four, and it cannot live in the wide. We are not stopping the set to build one.
